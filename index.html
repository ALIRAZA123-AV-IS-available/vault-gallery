Files live in this browser's IndexedDB on this device only. Clearing site data, using a different browser or profile, or some private-browsing modes will remove or hide them. Keep your originals elsewhere.

Security & privacy

  • This is a front-end privacy lock. It is not real authentication: anyone who can use this browser profile or open its developer tools can reach the stored files.
  • Media is not encrypted. A 4–9 tile sequence has too few possible combinations to protect an encryption key, so claiming encryption would be misleading.
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Cross-device access or real accounts would need a backend with authentication, authorization and server-side storage.

Reset

Resetting preferences restores theme, lock style, view and auto-lock to defaults. It does not touch your sequence or media.
